# Project imagery

Method: built-in image generation. Both assets are photorealistic illustrations, not documentary photographs of named people or organisations. No reference images were used. The generated PNG files are bundled unchanged.

Saved assets:

- `assets/project-worlds-1.png`: community video creators (top left), game developers (top right), robotics technicians (bottom left), VR architects (bottom right).
- `assets/project-worlds-2.png`: AI ethics team (top left), clean-energy designers (top right), coastal restoration team (bottom left), rooftop farming team (bottom right).

## Prompt set

Shared specification: photorealistic natural editorial photography for a classroom future-career website; a precise 2-by-2 atlas, overall landscape 3:2, four equal landscape tiles. Independent candid scenes of diverse young adult professionals, practical believable equipment, natural light, tactile materials and optimistic teamwork. Keep important action inside each tile. No labels, captions, logos, watermark, futuristic armour or holographic text.

Atlas 1: a community video-production team interviewing a volunteer in a bright filming studio; game artists collaborating around a colourful Istanbul-inspired exploration game, with a controller and sketchbook; robotics technicians assembling a small wheeled service robot in a practical workshop; VR designers testing an immersive museum, with a headset user and a museum scene on a screen.

Atlas 2: an AI ethics team comparing sample outputs and simple charts around a laptop; clean-energy designers inspecting school rooftop solar panels with a tablet; a Mediterranean coastal restoration team collecting plastic with reusable bags and recording observations; urban farmers tending leafy vegetables in rooftop raised beds, with a city skyline and watering can.
