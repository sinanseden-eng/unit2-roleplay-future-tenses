THE MISSION

One team. One shared story.

Your group of three shares one profession and one project. You are partners, not three unrelated characters. Your project started in 2032. At your reunion in 2035, discuss your plans for the next ten years, up to 2045.

Every member contributes, uses a different target form and responds to someone else. Finish with a challenge question from the screen and one follow-up question from another group.
