2035 Virtual Reunion

An interactive role-play challenge to practice future tenses.

Your Mission

Scenario: It is 2035 and you are meeting your college friends at a virtual reunion. You haven't connected since graduation.

Goal: Each member of your group must use at least **one different future tense** with ‘will’ during your conversation. Introduce yourselves based on your new role. Then, discuss what you think you will have achieved or will be doing in the next 10 years (by 2045).

How to Perform as a Group (Groups of 3)

Your group of three shares **one secret identity**. You are partners in the same profession.

Preparation Steps:

  1. Discuss Your Story: How do you work together? Did you start a company? What is your company's name?
  2. Assign Roles: Each person must choose one of the following roles for the performance.
  3. Plan Your Performance: Decide who will say what. Each person must use a different future tense!
  4. Prepare for Questions: Be ready to answer a random question from the screen AND a follow-up question from another group after your performance.

Performance Roles & Examples:

Student 1: The Visionary (Focus on big dreams & long-term goals)

"I believe our company **will be** the biggest in the world. By 2045, we **will have been changing** the industry for over 20 years!"

Student 2: The Planner (Focus on completed actions & deadlines)

"That's right. And according to my schedule, by the end of next year, we **will have opened** our new office in Tokyo."

Student 3: The Realist (Focus on actions in progress)

"And while they plan, I **will be managing** the day-to-day operations. This time next month, I **will be hiring** our new team of engineers."

Voting Criteria

As you watch each group, consider these points before you vote:

  • Correct Grammar: Did the group use the future tenses correctly?
  • Variety of Tenses: Did each member use a *different* future tense?
  • Creativity & Performance: Was their story creative and their performance engaging?
  • Fluency: Did they speak fluently and confidently?
